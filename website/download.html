Installing it

  1. 1
    Tap Download Keepsake. Your phone saves the file.
  2. 2
    Open it from the notification, or from your Downloads.
  3. 3
    Allow it once. Android asks whether to allow installing from your browser, because this did not come from the Play Store.
  4. 4
    Tap Install, then open Keepsake and sign in.

Your phone may say the file is not from the Play Store. That shows for anything installed this way.

If it says the app cannot be installed, remove any older Keepsake from your phone first, then open the file again. Anything you had saved on the phone is kept in the app's own storage only, so start fresh if you were only trying it out.
