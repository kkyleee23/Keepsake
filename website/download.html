Get Keepsake on Android

One file, one tap. If you would rather not install anything, Keepsake also works in your browser.

Download Keepsake

Works on every Android phone. __SIZE_UNIVERSAL__ to download.

Or open it in your browser, with nothing to install.

Installing it

  1. Tap Download Keepsake. Your phone saves the file.
  2. Open it, from the notification or from your Downloads.
  3. Android asks whether to allow installing from your browser, because this did not come from the Play Store. Say yes.
  4. Tap Install, then open Keepsake.

Your phone may warn you that the file is not from the Play Store. That warning shows for anything installed this way.

Other versions

The download above fits every phone, which is why it is the larger one. These are each built for one kind of phone, so they are smaller. Pick one only if you know which you need.

  • Keepsake for arm64 Almost every phone sold since about 2017. __SIZE_ARM64__.
  • Keepsake for arm32 Older and smaller phones. __SIZE_ARM32__.
  • Keepsake for x86-64 Chromebooks and emulators, rarely a phone. __SIZE_X64__.

Questions

Is there an iPhone version? Not yet. Keepsake works in Safari in the meantime, and the person you send a keepsake to never needs the app at all.

Does the person I send it to need this? No. They open your link in whatever browser they already have.
