How it goes

Four steps. Nothing for them to set up.

  1. 1 Pick what it’s for

    A birthday, an apology, a hello from far away, or no reason at all. Each one gives you a first line to change or throw away.

  2. 2 Write it your way

    A short note or a long letter. Choose the paper (cream, kraft, linen, seed) and whether it looks typed or handwritten.

  3. 3 Wrap it up

    Pick the envelope and the wax seal, then stick on a stamp, a pressed sprig or the pup. Drag them wherever you like.

  4. 4 Send the link

    By message, email or a QR code. They see your envelope first, then the seal breaks and the letter slides out.

What they get

Everything you put in, laid out like paper on a table: a letter in your handwriting, a little book that turns its pages, photos with white borders, a list of reasons, notes to open later.

They can send back a reaction or a few words. Only you can read them, and the app shows you when new ones come in.

Private by default

Keepsake is for things you’d only say to one person. Here is what that means in practice.

22 random characters
That’s every link. There’s no list of keepsakes to browse and nothing to guess.
5 wrong PINs
Add a PIN, and five wrong guesses lock it for 15 minutes, then longer each time, up to a day.
Stop sharing anytime
The link stops working on the spot and the copy on our server is deleted. That link can never be used again, by anyone.
Sealed until the day
Pick a date and the server won’t hand it over before then, to anyone, however they ask.
No location in photos
Photos are shrunk and their location and camera details removed on your phone, before they’re sent.
0 trackers
No ads, no analytics, no third-party scripts. Not on this page, not in the app.
